Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

We keep this short and human, because that's how we'd want to read it. The gist: we collect only what we need to get good things to you, we don't sell your information, and you can reach a real person any time at hello@garts-shoppe.example.

What we collect

  • Order details — your name, shipping and billing address, email, and what you bought, so we can pack it, ship it, and help if something goes wrong.
  • Payment information — handled by our payment processor. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Contact messages — if you email us, we keep the conversation so we can follow up.
  • Basic site data — standard analytics like which pages get visited, to make the shop work better. Nothing creepy.

How we use it

To fulfill your orders, answer your questions, process returns and replacements, and — only if you opt in — send the occasional note about restocks or new decor drops. We don't send pressure emails or fake countdowns; that's just not us. You can unsubscribe from marketing at any time with one click.

Who we share it with

Only the partners who help us run the shop: our payment processor, shipping carriers, and the services that host the site and handle email. They get only what they need to do their job, and they're bound to protect it. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to keep your cart working and to understand general site traffic. You can decline non-essential cookies and the shop will still work fine.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Just email hello@garts-shoppe.example and we'll take care of it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat your information the way we'd want ours treated.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top. For anything unclear, write to us — a person will answer.
