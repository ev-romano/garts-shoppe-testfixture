Questions, answered honestly

If your question isn't here, email a real person at hello@garts-shoppe.example. We'd rather over-explain than have you guess.

Why does a planter cost $42 when the hardware store sells one for $9?

Fair question, and we'd ask it too. The short answer: drainage that actually works, a matching saucer so your shelf stays dry, real stoneware that holds its glaze instead of flaking, and a shape we obsessed over so it looks like something, not nothing. The cheap pot is cheaper because someone paid for it later — usually you, when it cracks. Our whole thing is buy the right thing once. If that's not where you are right now, no hard feelings.

Will my stoneware actually survive shipping?

This is our most-asked question and the one we lose the most sleep over. Every ceramic piece ships double-boxed, cradled in molded pulp trays shaped to the curves — no plastic, no loose peanuts. The overwhelming majority arrive perfect. If yours doesn't, see the next answer; we make it right, fast.

What if it arrives cracked or chipped?

We replace anything damaged in transit, and for your first report we don't even ask for a photo — just email hello@garts-shoppe.example with your order number and what happened. A replacement goes out. Full details on the returns page.

Do your planters have drainage?

Yes. The Stoneware Planter has a real drainage hole and comes with a matching saucer to catch the runoff. Drainage is the single biggest thing that keeps a plant alive, so we weren't going to skip it to save a step. If you want to keep a plant in a decorative cover pot with no hole, use a plastic nursery pot inside and lift it out to water — never let roots sit in standing water.

Are your planters safe around my cat / dog / kid?

The pots themselves are just stoneware — heavy, stable, nothing toxic. The thing to watch is the plant you put in them. We sell pots, not plants, and quite a few popular houseplants (pothos, lilies, aloe, philodendron and others) are toxic to pets if chewed. Before you plant, look up your specific plant against a pet-safe list — the ASPCA keeps a good one — or ask us and we'll point you toward safe, easy options like spider plants, herbs, or calatheas. A heavy pot also helps: it's harder for a curious pet to tip.

Is this just decor, or will I actually use it?

Both, and we care about the second part more. The Woven Tray corrals keys and mail. The vase holds the grocery-store flowers you keep meaning to buy. The tools get dirt under your nails every weekend. We design for "lived with," not "looked at." If a thing only earns its keep as decoration, that's fine too — but most of ours quietly become useful.

When will the sold-out decor come back?

Honestly, sometimes never. Our decor drops are small-batch and limited on purpose — we'd rather make a lovely run of a few hundred than a warehouse of sameness. We won't put a fake countdown timer on you or spam "only 2 left!" nonsense. If you want to know about a specific piece, email us and we'll tell you the truth about whether it's likely to return.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

If it hasn't shipped, almost always yes — email hello@garts-shoppe.example as soon as you can with your order number. We pack quickly (partly so fragile things spend less time in transit), so the sooner you reach us the better. Once it's on the truck we'll help you with a return instead.

Do you gift wrap?

We don't offer formal gift wrap yet, but everything arrives in clean, plastic-free packaging that looks nice as-is, and we can leave off the receipt and add a short note if you tell us at checkout or by email. The Balcony Tool Set and a planter make a genuinely good "get into gardening" gift.

Help — I keep killing my plants. Any advice?

You're in good company, and it's usually one of two things: too much water or not enough light. Start with our plant basics page and the beginner post on the blog. Short version: pick a plant that matches the light you actually have, use a pot with drainage (ours qualify), and let the top inch of soil dry before watering again.

Where do you ship, and how much?

Free shipping in the US on orders over $50, otherwise a flat $6. Canada is a flat $15. We don't ship stoneware outside North America — it's too fragile to trust to a long haul, and we'd rather say no than send you a box of shards. Full breakdown on the shipping page.

How should I clean and care for my stoneware?

Gentle is the rule — sudden temperature swings are what crack ceramic, not everyday use. Full instructions, plus how to sharpen your snips and oil the ash tool handles, live on the care page.

What's the "second-life shelf"?

When a return comes back to us in good shape, we don't landfill it — we clean it up, mark it down, and offer it on our second-life discount shelf. Same quality, smaller price, one less good thing thrown away. It's some of our favorite inventory.
