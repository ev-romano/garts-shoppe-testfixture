Delivery times

We pack orders within 1–2 business days — partly so fragile pieces spend as little time in transit as possible. Once it's moving, US delivery is typically 3–6 business days depending on where you are; Canada runs a bit longer, usually 6–10 business days plus any time at customs. You'll get tracking by email the moment your box ships.

Why we don't ship stoneware overseas

We'd love to, and we get asked often. But a long international haul with many hand-offs is exactly the journey that turns a beautiful planter into a box of pieces. Rather than take your money and cross our fingers, we've drawn the line at North America for anything ceramic. If you're outside that and want a non-fragile item, email us and we'll see what's sensible.

How we pack fragile things

This is where we put the care. Every stoneware and ceramic piece is:

  • Double-boxed — an inner box cradled inside an outer box, so no drop lands directly on the product.
  • Cushioned in molded pulp — trays shaped to hug the curves of each piece, holding it still through the bumps.
  • Plastic-free — no bubble wrap, no foam peanuts scattering across your floor. The pulp is recyclable or compostable, and the boxes are worth keeping.

The result: the overwhelming majority of stoneware arrives perfect. When something does get hurt in transit, we replace it — no photo needed the first time. See returns for how.

A note on the second-life shelf

Items from our second-life discount shelf ship with the same careful packing as everything else. A markdown never means a shortcut on protection.
