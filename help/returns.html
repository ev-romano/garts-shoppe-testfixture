Returns & the Second-Life Shelf

We want you to keep what you love and send back what isn't working, with as little fuss as possible. Here's how it works.

Our 30-day promise

Changed your mind? You have 30 days from delivery to return an item in its original condition for a refund. No restocking fees, no interrogation about why. We ask only that it come back unused and repackable so it can find a good second home.

Arrived damaged? We fix it fast.

Ceramic travels a long way. If something shows up cracked, chipped, or broken, we'll replace it. For your first damage report we won't ask for a photo — we take your word for it. Just email hello@garts-shoppe.example with your order number and a sentence about what happened, and a replacement goes out. (For repeat reports on the same order we may ask for a quick photo, only so we can improve our packing.)

How to start a return

  1. Email hello@garts-shoppe.example with your order number and which item(s) you're returning.
  2. We'll reply with a prepaid label or the return address, whichever fits your situation.
  3. Repack the item — the original box and pulp trays are ideal, so hang onto them until you're sure.
  4. Drop it off, and we'll refund the original payment method once it's back with us and checked in.

Refunds usually land within a few business days of us receiving the item. We'll email you when it's on its way.

The second-life discount shelf

Here's what happens to the good things that come back. When a return arrives in solid shape — and most do — we don't throw it away or quietly bin it. We clean it up, inspect it, mark it down, and offer it on our second-life shelf. You get a genuinely lovely piece for less; a perfectly good object stays out of a landfill. It's some of the inventory we're proudest of, and it sells out quickly for good reason. Keep an eye on the shop.

A few honest limits

  • Limited small-batch decor is still returnable within 30 days, but once a run is gone we can't always offer an identical replacement — a refund may be the only option.
  • Items returned used, dirty, or missing parts may receive a partial refund. We'll always talk it through with you first.
  • Return shipping on a change-of-mind return may be deducted; damaged-in-transit replacements are always on us.
